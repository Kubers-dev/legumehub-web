LegumeGenomics
Tennessee State University

We develop genomic resources, bioinformatics tools, and transcriptomic atlases to accelerate gene discovery, candidate gene prioritization, and genomics-assisted breeding in legume crops.

Explore Our Tools Our Research
What we do
Research Focus
Our work bridges plant molecular genetics, functional genomics, and computational biology to build tools and knowledge that advance crop improvement.
🧬

Functional Genomics

Large-scale transcriptomic atlas development for legume crops including chickpea and soybean, covering tissue-specific gene expression across developmental stages.

🌱

GWAS and Molecular Breeding

Genome-wide association studies to identify SNP-trait associations and prioritize candidate genes for agronomic and nutritional traits in legumes.

💻

Bioinformatics Tools

Development of interactive web platforms for gene expression visualization, GWAS candidate gene exploration, and functional annotation anchored to community-standard reference genomes.

🔬

Plant Transformation

CRISPR-based genome editing and plant transformation strategies to functionally validate candidate genes identified through genomic and transcriptomic approaches.
